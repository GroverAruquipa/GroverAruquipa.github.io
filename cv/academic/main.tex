% Research Interests section
\ruleline{\textbf{Research Interests}}
\footnotesize
My research develops geometric and kinematic foundations for advanced parallel robots with \textbf{kinematic redundancy}, \textbf{unlimited rotations}, and \textbf{integrated grasping}. I combine screw theory, Lie group methods, and higher-order mobility analysis with hardware prototyping and real-time experimental validation. I am increasingly interested in bridging classical kinematic rigor with modern manipulation learning through geometric priors and differentiable kinematic models.
\normalsize
