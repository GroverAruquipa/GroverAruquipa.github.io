Senior Robotics Engineer, Manipulation

Dear Hiring Team,

I grew up in Bolivia, a country where much of the economy still runs on mining and where most people know someone who does hard, risky physical work for a living. When I read that RoboForce builds robots for dull, dirty and dangerous work, starting with sites like solar farms and mines, it was the first time a company described the reason I went into robotics in its own words. That is the main reason I am applying, and I would be proud to work on it.

For the last three years I have built manipulators at Universit\'e Laval with Prof.\ Cl\'ement Gosselin. I designed, fabricated and controlled three of them, with 4, 7 and 8 actuators, from the kinematic model and CAD to real-time control at 500\,Hz on the physical robot. One of them, a coaxial wrist-gripper, turns its tool through ten continuous revolutions with sub-degree tracking while it keeps holding the object. I work daily with rigid-body kinematics, dynamics and Lie groups, and I have used real-time C++, MoveIt, Pinocchio and MuJoCo along the way.

TITAN's five primitives caught my attention because they match what I have been working on. Pick is the grasp, which in my robots is built into the mechanism itself. Twist is unlimited rotation of the held object, the main topic of my PhD. Press and connect are interaction problems, where kinematics, stiffness and control have to work together on real hardware, which is the part of robotics I enjoy most.

I defend my PhD in January 2027 and could relocate to Milpitas after that. I would need visa sponsorship, which I understand RoboForce offers. I would be glad to talk about how I could help the arm on TITAN reach, grasp and turn things reliably in the field.

Thank you for your time.

Sincerely,\\
Grover Aruquipa
