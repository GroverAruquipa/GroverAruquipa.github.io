Dear Physical AI Team,

I am applying for the Physical AI Research Scientist position. I am finishing my PhD in Mechanical Engineering at Universit\'e Laval with Prof.\ Cl\'ement Gosselin and Prof.\ Patrice Lambert, and I defend in early 2027. What attracts me to Vention is that your robots go to real factories, and that you want to bring learning-based manipulation to the factory floor.

During my PhD I designed, built and controlled three manipulators with integrated grippers, with 4, 7 and 8 actuators. I did each one from the kinematic model to the working robot: the design, the fabrication, the wiring and the real-time control at 500\,Hz, and then the debugging on the bench until it worked. Before running anything on hardware I validate the models and trajectories in MuJoCo. Right now I am training a reinforcement learning grasping policy in NVIDIA Isaac Sim for a Delta robot with an integrated gripper that I am building, and taking it from simulation to the real robot is the next step.

I also bring the software and machine learning side. I program in Python and C++, I have used PyTorch, and before my PhD I worked on computer vision for industrial anomaly detection at Sencon and on machine learning for high-voltage circuit breakers at Hitachi ABB Power Grids R\&D in Zurich. I like working with people from other fields: I supervise two graduate students day to day, and I led the Laval team that won first place in the ASME Student Mechanisms and Robotics Design Competition in 2025.

I am available from January 2027 and ready to move to Montr\'eal. I will be eligible for an open Post-Graduation Work Permit, so I do not need sponsorship. You can see my robots on my website, groveraruquipa.github.io.

Thank you for your time,
