Dear Enact team,

I am applying for the Robotic Controls Engineer position. I am finishing my PhD in Mechanical Engineering at Universit\'e Laval with Prof.\ Cl\'ement Gosselin and Prof.\ Patrice Lambert, and I defend in early 2027. Your idea of giving robots a whole-body sense of force through their own structure and actuators is very close to what I have been working on, and it is the main reason I am writing to you.

During my PhD I brought up three manipulators with integrated grippers, with 4, 7 and 8 actuators. For each one I wrote the kinematic and dynamic model, designed and built the robot, wired the motors, drives and encoders, and wrote the real-time control at 500\,Hz. Then came the part I like most: debugging on the bench until the robot did what the model said, and finding out each time whether the problem was the model, the gains, the electronics or a part that was flexing. I write the URDF and MJCF files of my robots myself and validate them in MuJoCo before anything runs on hardware.

My current robot is a Delta with an integrated Sarrus gripper, designed to sense the grasp force through the motor currents instead of using a sensor in the fingers. I am training a reinforcement learning policy for it in NVIDIA Isaac Sim, and taking it from simulation to the real robot is my next step. That is the same kind of problem your team works on every day, and I would like to solve it with you.

I program in Python and C++ and have used PyTorch. I also know Z\"urich: I did an internship at Hitachi ABB Power Grids R\&D there in 2020, working on machine learning for sensor data. I am available from January 2027 and ready to relocate and work on site. Prof.\ Gosselin, Prof.\ Lambert and Univ.-Prof.\ Andreas M\"uller of JKU Linz can speak to my work, and you can see my robots on my website.

Thank you for your time,
