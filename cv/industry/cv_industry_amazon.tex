%================ SUMMARY ================
\section{Summary}
Mechanical engineer finishing a PhD in robot mechanisms and grasping (Universit\'e Laval, defense early 2027). I take
robotic hardware from specification and concept through SolidWorks design, FEA, prototyping, validation and testing:
three multi-DoF robots built, a fourth in progress that picks fragile objects quickly by estimating grasp force from
motor currents. My first robot, during my bachelor's, was a reconfigurable Delta for product picking. Industry
experience in computer vision and data science.

%================ SKILLS ================
\section{Technical Skills}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Mechanical design:} SolidWorks (expert, advanced multi-body assemblies and configurations), Creo; robot
mechanisms, actuation and motor selection, transmissions, integrated grippers; DFx (design for manufacturing, assembly
and test).
\item \textbf{Analysis:} FEA in ANSYS; statics, dynamics, kinematics; MATLAB, Simulink and Simscape; MuJoCo, NVIDIA Isaac
Sim, Pinocchio.
\item \textbf{Prototyping and validation:} proof-of-concept models and prototypes (machining, SLS and FDM printing),
functional specifications, design verification and test procedures, data analysis; real-time control at 500\,Hz.
\item \textbf{Software and electronics:} Python, C++, ROS and ROS~2, OpenCV, PyTorch; Altium (PCB), motor drives and
encoder wiring.
\end{itemize}

%================ EXPERIENCE ================
\section{Experience}
\role{Doctoral Researcher}{Robotics Laboratory, Universit\'e Laval}{Qu\'ebec, Canada}{2023 -- present}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Owned three robots end to end} (4, 7 and 8 actuators, integrated grippers): specification, concept,
SolidWorks design, component selection, prototyping, assembly, validation testing and redesign.
\item \textbf{Delta robot with integrated Sarrus gripper (in progress):} built to pick fragile objects such as eggs at
speed, with grasp force estimated from motor currents instead of a force sensor.
\item \textbf{4-DoF coaxial wrist-gripper:} four actuation channels in concentric shafts, 0.25\,kg moving mass; verified
over ten continuous revolutions with sub-degree tracking while holding the object.
\item Planned the tests and analyzed the results for each prototype; worked across mechanics, electronics and software;
mentored two graduate students; first author of 3 journal papers and 4 under review.
\end{itemize}
