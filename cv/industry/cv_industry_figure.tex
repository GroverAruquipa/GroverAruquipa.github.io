%================ SUMMARY ================
\section{Summary}
Robotics engineer finishing a PhD (Universit\'e Laval, defense early 2027) who brings multi-DoF robots from first
assembly to validated motion. I have integrated and brought up three robots with 4, 7 and 8 actuators and integrated
grippers: wiring motors, drives and encoders, writing the real-time control in C++ and Simulink at 500\,Hz, tuning the
controllers, and debugging mechanical, electrical and software faults on the bench until each robot met its targets.
I am now integrating a gripper that estimates grasp force from motor currents, without a sensor in the fingers.

%================ SKILLS ================
\section{Technical Skills}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Integration and bring-up:} motors, drives and incremental encoders; position and current sensing; first
power-up and commissioning of multi-DoF robots; root-cause debugging across mechanics, electronics and software.
\item \textbf{Real-time control:} multi-axis control at 500\,Hz (OPAL-RT RT-LAB, Simulink Real-Time); C++ (real time),
Python, MATLAB and Simulink; controller tuning; force estimation from motor currents; diagnostics and data analysis.
\item \textbf{Robotics:} kinematics, dynamics, Jacobians and singularity monitoring for redundant manipulators and grippers;
ROS and ROS~2, MoveIt, Pinocchio; simulation in MuJoCo, NVIDIA Isaac Sim and Simscape.
\item \textbf{Test and hardware:} test setups for tracking accuracy, repeatability and continuous rotation; SolidWorks,
ANSYS; machining, 3D printing and assembly of prototypes; Altium (PCB); Linux, Git; lab equipment.
\end{itemize}
