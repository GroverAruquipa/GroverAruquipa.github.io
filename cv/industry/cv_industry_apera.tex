%================ SUMMARY ================
\section{Summary}
Mechatronics engineer finishing a PhD (Universit\'e Laval, defense early 2027) who designs and builds robots with
integrated grasping, hands-on and hardware-first. I designed, built and brought up three robots with 4, 7 and 8 actuators
and integrated grippers: SolidWorks design, motor, encoder and gearbox selection, wiring, real-time C++ control at 500\,Hz,
Python tests and debugging on the bench. Before my PhD I worked as a computer vision engineer on industrial vision.
Eligible for an open Post-Graduation Work Permit in Canada, no sponsorship required.

%================ SKILLS ================
\section{Technical Skills}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Mechanical design:} SolidWorks; mechanisms, grippers, transmissions, mounts and assemblies; 3D printing and
assembly of prototypes; design iterations driven by bench tests.
\item \textbf{Electrical and integration:} motor, encoder and gearbox selection; wiring of motors, drives and encoders;
current sensing; bring-up of multi-axis robots; tracing failures across mechanics, electronics and software; Altium (PCB).
\item \textbf{Software:} Python (test scripts, data analysis, tooling); C++ (real time); MATLAB and Simulink; multi-axis
control at 500\,Hz (OPAL-RT RT-LAB, Simulink Real-Time); Linux, Git.
\item \textbf{Robotics and vision:} kinematics, Jacobians and singularity analysis; motion and trajectory planning;
ROS and ROS~2, MoveIt, Pinocchio; MuJoCo and NVIDIA Isaac Sim; industrial computer vision.
\end{itemize}

%================ EXPERIENCE ================
\section{Experience}
\role{Doctoral Researcher}{Robotics Laboratory, Universit\'e Laval}{Qu\'ebec, Canada}{2023 -- present}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Designed, built and brought up three robots with integrated grippers} (4, 7 and 8 actuators): concept,
SolidWorks design, actuator and sensor selection, wiring, real-time control, tuning and validation on the bench.
\item \textbf{4-DoF coaxial wrist-gripper:} four actuation channels through concentric shafts in a 180\,mm footprint;
measured ten continuous revolutions with sub-degree tracking error at 500\,Hz while the gripper held the object.
\item \textbf{Delta robot with integrated Sarrus gripper (in progress):} grasp force estimated from motor currents,
without a sensor in the fingers, to pick fragile objects without crushing them.
\item \textbf{Simulation before hardware:} every kinematic model and trajectory validated in MuJoCo before running on the
real robot. Day-to-day lead of two graduate students on an 8-actuator robot.
\end{itemize}
