%================ SUMMARY ================
\section{Summary}
Mechanical engineer finishing a PhD (Universit\'e Laval, defense January 2027) on the hardware design of non-traditional
multi-DoF robots. I have personally taken three robots with unusual kinematic structures from concept and CAD through
component selection, fabrication, wiring and real-time control to working, validated prototypes. The most compact is a
coaxial wrist-gripper that rolls its tool without limit while actuating the jaw through concentric shafts, the same
function a surgical instrument needs.

%================ SKILLS ================
\section{Technical Skills}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Mechanism design:} parallel and hybrid mechanisms with unusual kinematic structures, coaxial and concentric
transmissions (hollow shafts, belts), revolute and prismatic joints, integrated grippers, vendor component selection.
\item \textbf{CAD, fabrication and electronics:} SolidWorks, Creo, ANSYS; machining, SLS and FDM printing, assembly and
commissioning of prototypes; Altium (basic PCB design), motor and encoder wiring.
\item \textbf{Kinematics and dynamics:} rigid-body kinematics and dynamics, screw theory and Lie groups, Jacobians,
singularity and workspace analysis, simulation in MATLAB and MuJoCo.
\item \textbf{Control and software:} real-time multi-axis control at 500\,Hz (OPAL-RT, Simulink Real-Time), C++ (real
time), Python, MATLAB, ROS~2, MoveIt, Pinocchio.
\end{itemize}

%================ EXPERIENCE ================
\section{Experience}
\role{Doctoral Researcher}{Robotics Laboratory, Universit\'e Laval}{Qu\'ebec, Canada}{2023 -- present}
\begin{itemize}
\item Designed and built, from concept to functional prototype, three non-traditional multi-DoF robots: kinematically
redundant parallel and hybrid mechanisms with 4, 7 and 8 base-mounted actuators and a gripper driven through the mechanism.
\item 4-DoF coaxial wrist-gripper: four actuation channels through concentric hollow shafts, non-backdrivable gripper,
180\,mm footprint, 0.25\,kg moving mass; unlimited tool-axis roll validated over ten continuous revolutions with
sub-degree tracking under 500\,Hz real-time control.
\item Designed the revolute and prismatic joints and transmissions, selected motors, encoders and drives, and machined,
printed, wired and commissioned each prototype myself.
\item Analyzed kinematics, dynamics, singularities and workspace in MATLAB and MuJoCo; derived closed-form inverse
kinematics and a redundancy strategy that keeps unlimited rotation free of collisions and singularities.
\item Led two graduate students from concept to prototype on an 8-actuator (6+2)-DoF robot. First author of 3 journal papers (ASME J.\ Mechanisms and Robotics, Mechanism and Machine Theory) and 4 more
under review (full list on page 2); reviewer for ASME J.\ Mechanisms and Robotics.
\end{itemize}
